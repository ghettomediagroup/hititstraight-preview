Lessons, Mentorship, and a Way to Give Back

Lessons at Ravisloe. Mentorship in the community. Three ways to be part of it.

Hit It Straight Golf Academy is run by Dion Madkins. He teaches kids and adults at Ravisloe Country Club in Homewood, Illinois, and he is the founder of the nonprofit behind it.

For Kids

Your child learns the game from a teaching professional. They also learn to focus, to keep their head up after a bad shot, and to handle themselves on a course where etiquette matters.

For Adults

Adults learn here too. Dion is a certified club fitter and a certified club smith, so ask about your clubs when you call.

For Neighbors Who Want to Help

We are a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. EIN 86-2045382. We go into underprivileged and underserved communities and teach kids golf, life skills, and mentorship. Your gift helps keep that work going.

Meet Coach Dion Madkins

Founder and CEO. Seen on Golf Channel in 2019.

Golf Channel segment, 2019 This clip will sit here. It does not play yet: embedding is restricted on the Vimeo account that hosts it, which is not owned by the academy. It does not currently play on hititstraightgolfacademy.com either.

Dion started in golf at twelve years old, as a caddie. He lives in Woodlawn, on the South Side of Chicago, and he credits the game with pulling him out of trouble as a young man. People told him he had the talent to chase a tour card. He chose to stay home and teach instead, because touring would have taken him away from his wife and child. He has been playing and teaching for about twenty years.
