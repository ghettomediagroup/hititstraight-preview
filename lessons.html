Who You Learn From

Dion Madkins started in golf as a 12 year old caddie. He lives in Woodlawn on the South Side of Chicago, and he credits the game with pulling him out of trouble as a kid. Before Your First Lesson

How Dion teaches, and what to bring.

No two students walk in with the same swing or the same reason for being there, so no two plans look the same. You also do not need a perfect set of clubs to start. Dion is a certified club fitter and a certified club smith, so if what you are swinging is wrong for you, he will say so. That matters most for kids, who often end up with adult clubs that are too long and too heavy for them.

  • First you talk about what you want to learn and what is getting in the way.
  • Then you set goals together.
  • Then you go to work, and the next lesson builds on the last one.
  • Bring water and sun protection for lessons held outdoors.
  • Clubs: NEEDED: do you keep loaner clubs on hand, and does a new student need to bring their own to a first lesson
  • What to wear: NEEDED: the dress code at Ravisloe, and whether golf shoes are required
  • Range balls: NEEDED: are range balls included in the lesson price, or bought separately
  • Juniors: NEEDED: may a parent or guardian stay and watch a junior lesson
  • Weather: NEEDED: what happens when a lesson is rained out, and how students are told
  • Season: NEEDED: do you teach year round, and if not, which months
  • Where to meet: NEEDED: where at Ravisloe a new student should meet Dion, and where to park
