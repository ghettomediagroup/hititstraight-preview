Our Mission

Hit It Straight Golf Academy, also called H.I.S.G.A., a 501(c)(3) not-for-profit organization.

We go into underprivileged and underserved communities and not only teach kids how to play golf. We also teach life skills and mentorship as well.

That is the whole idea. A young person who learns this game learns to slow down, think a shot through, and own the result either way. We bring that to young people who have not had a way in.

Who We Serve

Young people who have not had a way into this game, and adults too.

Our home course is Ravisloe Country Club in Homewood. Dion Madkins, our founder, is a Woodlawn resident who started in this game at 12 years old, carrying bags as a caddie. We teach adults as well as kids. If you have questions about lessons for your child or for yourself, call (708) 323-9980 or send us a note on the lessons page.

What Golf Asks of You

No clock, no teammate, no hiding.

There is no clock to run out and no teammate to cover for you. You call your own penalties. A young person feels that the first time they play a hole, and it tends to stay with them.

  • Discipline. You practice the boring part until the hard part gets easier.
  • Composure. You hit a bad shot, let it go, and play the next one clean.
  • Sportsmanship. Golfers call penalties on themselves, even when nobody is watching.

The Barrier Is Access, Not Talent

Golf is where the introductions happen.

Scholarships. Internships. Mentors. Business relationships. A lot of that starts on a golf course, and it always has.

Getting into the game is the hard part. Clubs cost money. Range time costs money. Getting to a course costs somebody an afternoon. Those are the real reasons a young person never starts. That is the barrier we work on.

How We Work

What we focus on.

Show up and you get a coach. Here is what that means in practice.

  • Instruction. Coaching from a Callaway-sponsored teaching professional with about 20 years of playing and teaching behind him. We start by finding out what the student wants to learn, set goals together, and build from there.
  • Mentorship. The golf is the reason they show up. The conversation on the walk between shots is the reason it sticks.
  • Clubs. Dion is a certified club fitter and a certified club smith, so he knows how a club is supposed to fit the person swinging it.
  • Course time. Ravisloe Country Club in Homewood is a public course designed by Donald Ross. It is our home course.

Why This Work Exists

Dion Madkins, Founder and CEO

Dion started in golf at 12 years old, carrying bags as a caddie. He credits the game with pulling him out of trouble as a kid. People told him he had the talent to turn pro. He chose to teach instead, because touring would have taken him away from his wife and child. About 20 years later he is still teaching, and still working with young people in underserved communities.
