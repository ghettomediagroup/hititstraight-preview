From caddie at 12 to coach

How golf found Dion, and why he stayed with it

Dion lives in Woodlawn, on the South Side of Chicago. He was 12 years old when he started caddying. The game got hold of him early, and by his own account it pulled him out of trouble he could have found instead.

He kept playing and he kept learning, and about 20 years later he's still doing both. People told him he had the talent to make it as a pro. He has a wife and a child, and touring would have taken him away from them, so he chose to teach instead.

His process with every student is the same. He finds out what the student actually wants to learn, sets goals around it, and moves forward from there. He works with students of all ages.

Questions parents and students ask

Answered by Dion

  • What is your typical process for working with a student? First I figure out exactly what the student wants to learn. Then we set goals to reach that outcome and move forward from there.
  • What education and training do you have? I'm a certified club fitter and a certified club smith, with about 20 years playing and teaching the game.
  • How did you get started teaching? People always told me I had the talent to make it as a golf pro. I have a wife and a child, and I knew touring would take time away from them, so I went the golf instructor route.
  • What types of students have you worked with? Students as young as 2 years old, all the way up to Division 1 college golfers.
  • Share one of your success stories. One of my fondest memories is being part of helping two students earn Division 1 college scholarships.
  • What should I look for in a golf instructor? Interview wisely. Ask questions and learn what the instructor knows about the game. Just as important, make sure they can explain the game in language you understand.
